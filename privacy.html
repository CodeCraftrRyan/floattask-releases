Privacy policy

What FloatTask collects, what stays on your computer, and who else is involved.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The short version What we collect Google data AI features Payments Who we share data with Retention and deletion Security Children Changes Contact

The short version

  • Your tasks live on your computer. FloatTask stores your task list, timers and settings in a local file on your device. We don't upload it.
  • You sign in with Google. We keep your Google account id, name and email so your subscription and AI allowance follow you between computers.
  • Calendar is read-only and optional. If you connect it, FloatTask reads upcoming events on your device to show meetings and help you prepare. We don't store your calendar on our servers.
  • AI features send what they need, when you use them. The relevant tasks and calendar events go through our servers to Anthropic to generate a response. We keep counts, not content.
  • Stripe handles payment. We never see your card number.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling data. We don't use advertising or analytics trackers on the site or in the app.

What we collect

On your device (not sent to us)

Tasks, notes, due dates, projects, task types, timers, reminders, app settings, your Google sign-in tokens and a cached copy of your plan status. All of this is kept in FloatTask's data folder on your computer and leaves only in the cases described below.

Account information (stored on our servers)

When you sign in with Google we store your Google account identifier, email address and display name, your plan (trial, Pro, Pro+ or complimentary), when you last used FloatTask AI, and a per-day count of AI requests with their token usage and estimated cost. This is how we enforce fair-use limits and tie your subscription to your account.

Support messages

If you use the contact form we keep the message, your name and email, and the topic you chose so we can reply and keep track of bugs.

What we don't collect

We don't run analytics or advertising trackers, don't collect your IP address beyond what our hosting providers log for security, and don't read your files, screen or other apps. The only cookie-like storage on floattask.com is your light/dark preference.

Google data

FloatTask asks Google for two things, at two different times:

  • Sign-in (openid, email, profile) — your account id, email and name. Used to identify you to our billing and AI services.
  • Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly) — requested only when you click Connect calendar. Used to show upcoming events in the app, link tasks to meetings, send a reminder before a meeting, and let FloatTask AI suggest prep tasks or brief you on your day.

Calendar data is fetched directly from Google by the app on your computer and is not stored on our servers. When you ask FloatTask AI something that involves your calendar (for example "what should I prepare for today?"), the titles, times and attendee names of the relevant upcoming events are included in that request so the model can answer; they are processed to produce the response and are not retained by us afterwards. Calendar data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to train AI models.

Disconnecting Google in Settings revokes FloatTask's access at Google and deletes the tokens from your device. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions.

FloatTask's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI features

FloatTask AI (the chat panel, Add with AI, smart reminders and daily briefs) is powered by Anthropic's Claude models, called through FloatTask's own servers so you never need an API key. When you use an AI feature, the app sends the text you typed plus the context the feature needs — typically your current task list and, if connected, upcoming calendar events — to our server, which forwards it to Anthropic and returns the answer.

  • We record that a request happened (date, count, tokens used, estimated cost) to enforce plan limits. We do not store the content of your prompts or the responses.
  • Anthropic processes the content to generate a response under its commercial terms, which do not permit training on API data.
  • If you turn off AI features, or use your own Anthropic key under Advanced settings, nothing is sent to our AI servers.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Checkout and the billing portal open in your browser on Stripe's pages; your card details go directly to Stripe and never pass through FloatTask. We store your Stripe customer id, subscription id, plan, billing interval, renewal date and status so the app knows what you're entitled to.

Retention and deletion

  • Local data stays on your computer until you delete it or uninstall FloatTask (the data file is in FloatTask's application-data folder).
  • Account and subscription records are kept while you have an account. Daily AI usage counts are kept for up to 13 months for billing and abuse prevention.
  • Support messages are kept for up to 2 years.
  • To delete your account and everything we hold about it, contact us from the email you signed in with. We'll confirm within 30 days. Cancelling a subscription doesn't delete the account; deletion ends any active subscription.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Server-side data is stored with Supabase in the United States with access restricted to the service itself. Your Google tokens are stored only on your device. Sign-in to our services is verified against Google's public keys on every request. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you.

Children

FloatTask is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country) and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and note it in the app or on the site. Continued use after a change means you accept the new policy.
