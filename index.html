Stays on top, even over full-screen apps

One list, three sizes

As small or as big as the moment needs.

The widget, the pill and the full app are the same list. A change in one shows up in the others instantly.

The widget

Today's tasks, a timer on each one, and a box to add more. Drag it anywhere; drag its top edge to make it taller.

The pill

Collapse it to a small button that shows how many tasks are due and, if you like, the running timer.

⌘ ⇧ L to show or collapse

The full app

Plan the week in Dashboard, List, Board or Calendar view, with an AI assistant on the side.

⌘ ⇧ F to open

Add with AI

Type it the way you'd say it.

Write a quick note and press ⌘ ↵. FloatTask works out the title, due date, project and type, splits a list into separate tasks, and warns you when something is already on your list.

send budget draft to sam fri 3pm for q4 planning, use revised version w/ travel split out → “Send budget draft to Sam” · Friday 3:00 PM · Follow-up · Q4 planning

Nothing is saved until you click Add. Plain ↵ still adds the text exactly as typed.

Plan the week

See the whole week, then get back to the corner.

The full app has a dashboard of what's due and overdue, time logged by project, and your upcoming meetings.

  • Dashboard, List, Board and Calendar views of the same tasks
  • Drag cards between columns or onto a day in the calendar
  • Ask the assistant to plan your afternoon or catch what slipped

A timer on every task

One click to start. Time adds up by project, and an optional bell rings every 30 min so the hours don't slip by.

Ready for your meetings

Connect Google Calendar to see what's next, link tasks to meetings, and get suggested prep tasks.

A nudge the day before

A notification 24 h before anything is due, written to tell you what actually needs doing.

Out of the way when you need it

Shrinks to the pill when another app goes full-screen, and comes back when you leave.

Light and dark

Follows your system setting, or pick one. Readable text sizes in both.

Your tasks stay on your computer

Tasks are stored locally. AI is built in — sign in with Google and it's on, no API key needed.
