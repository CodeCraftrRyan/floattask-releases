On top of your work

Why I made this

I love task managers. None of them ever worked for me.

My job is never a straight line, and I kept bending my work to fit the app instead of the other way round. Boards felt like clutter. And the moment I switched windows, the list was gone, and my focus went with it.

So I built the one I wanted: today's goals, and a timer for the thing I'm doing right now, sitting on top of everything else on my screen. I use it every day. I hope it helps you too.

Ryan HavilandMaker of FloatTask

A small circle with the running time beside it. Click to pause, drag it anywhere. When an app goes full-screen, the widget tucks into it.

The whole day on one screen, for when you want to plan instead of do.

Pick a few goals. Give them your time.

The full app opens on My Day. One card holds the task you're on, with its timer and small steps. Everything else for today waits in a plain list, and your meetings sit on a timeline beside it.

  • The timer only counts up. Nothing ticks down at you.
  • Big tasks become small steps you finish one at a time.
  • Reminders at the time you set, and a nudge the day before something's due.
  • Wrap up day opens a short journal and sets up tomorrow.

Before you download.

Do I need a Google account?

Yes. You sign in with Google to start your trial, and your plan follows that account to every computer. Connecting Google Calendar is optional and read-only.

Where is my data kept?

Tasks, notes and timers are stored on your computer. When you use Assist, only the tasks and events it needs are sent to generate a reply, and FloatTask keeps counts, not content. No ads, and nothing is ever sold. The app itself has no analytics; this website uses Google Analytics, which you can turn off from Cookie settings. Details are in the privacy policy.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your plan starts on day 8 unless you cancel before then from Settings, and then there's no charge. You can switch plans or cancel at any time after that too.

Which computers does it run on?

macOS on Apple Silicon or Intel, and Windows 10 or 11. The app updates itself when a new version is out.
